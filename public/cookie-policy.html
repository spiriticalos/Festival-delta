What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember certain information about your visit.

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the site to function and cannot be disabled. They do not require your consent under GDPR.

Name Type Purpose Duration
connect.sid Session Keeps the admin area secure. Only active when an administrator is logged in. Regular visitors are not affected. 4 hours
cookie-consent localStorage Stores your cookie preference so the banner is not shown on every visit. Until cleared

Optional marketing cookies

With your consent ("Accept All"), this site may use the following marketing and analytics services. These are only activated if you choose to accept all cookies.

Service Type Purpose
Google Ads Marketing Remarketing — shows festival ads to people who have previously visited the site.
Google Analytics (GA4) Analytics Measures site traffic and visitor behaviour to help improve the experience.

If you choose "Necessary Only", none of the optional cookies above will be loaded.

Third-party embedded content

This site embeds a YouTube video (Edition 2025 section) and a Google Maps iframe (Venue section). These third parties may set their own cookies when you interact with that content. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies:

Google / YouTube Privacy Policy

Managing your consent

You can change your cookie preference at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or cookies for this site, then refreshing the page — the consent banner will reappear.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Deleting the session cookie will log out any active admin session. Visitor experience is not affected.
