Who We Are

Do you love art and beauty?

Welcome to The Bohemians — a music & travel festival set in one of the wildest and most poetic places in Europe, right where the river meets the sea.

For four days and four nights, we gather a like-minded community to celebrate music, nature, and the joy of being fully present.

Location

Where Does the Festival Take Place?

Sfântu Gheorghe, Danube Delta

A fishing village at the end of the oldest arm of the Danube, right where the river meets the Black Sea. You can't get here by car. And that's exactly what makes it special.

How to Get There?

By Car

Drive to Murighiol or Tulcea, then take a boat transfer lasting 45 to 90 minutes.

By Bus

Eurolines runs direct coaches to Murighiol. From there, take a boat to Sfântu Gheorghe.

"The boat ride isn't an inconvenience — it's the first part of the experience."

Off Stage

What to Do at the Festival

Boat Trips

Guided boat excursions through the Delta's canals in search of Europe's rarest birds.

Paddle Journeys

SUP sessions on calm lakes in the morning, before the music begins.

Village Walk

Sfântu Gheorghe is an old fishing village on the Sulina arm of the Danube, right where the river meets the sea. Worth exploring on foot.

Beach & Sunset

Wild beach, fine sand, sunset straight into the sea. No sunbeds, no crowds, no noise.
